Headline Takeaways

  • Community #5 is carrying everything: 365 of 387 messages (94%). This time Germany (142) and Canada (120) were busier than UK (86).
  • Activity per day almost doubled: about 97 messages a day, compared with 52 last report.
  • Peer guidance in #5: members explained German public universities (no tuition), DAAD for master's, blocked-account loans, Stipendium Hungaricum, KAIST in South Korea and research-based funded master's.
  • The older communities have gone quiet: #2 had no messages at all, #4 had 3, and #1's only activity was admins removing posts.
  • Short.io is picking up: 381 clicks in the week of 16 to 22 Nov (+31%), and 26 Nov had 90 clicks, the joint-highest day of the month.
  • 2 leads were added into the CRM this period.
Needs Attention
  • Accommodation lead: a student from Timor-Leste posted in #5 that she is "still looking for accommodation". A direct follow-up from the team could turn this into a booking.
  • Student still waiting in #5 Canada: after asking for guidance since early November, a member wrote "nobody is guiding" and tagged the admin.
  • Mixed Amber Scholar dates: in #4, a member told others results come out "in the middle of Feb", which doesn't match the 31 January the team shared earlier.
  • Moving conversations off the group: in #5 Germany a member offered guidance by DM, and in #3 UNSW students moved to an Instagram group chat.
